Untitled (after Prentententoonstelling), 2026

recursive photograph · live computation on glass

The photograph hangs inside itself: each copy is reduced and turned, so the picture recedes without ever reaching bottom. Escher bent the same recursion into Prentententoonstelling (1956), leaving a blank spot where the mathematics overwhelmed the hand. Here the blank is filled — the spiral is computed live, behind glass, for as long as you care to watch.
