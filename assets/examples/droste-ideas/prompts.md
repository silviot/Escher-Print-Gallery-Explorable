## Lighthouse

Create a singular surreal Droste artwork: "The lighthouse that lights itself". No people. Square composition.

A weather-beaten chalk-white lighthouse with an unmistakable vermilion roof and balcony stands on a small, jagged black basalt island. A steep pale staircase zigzags from a tiny red boathouse by the water up to the tower. Atlantic waves wrap the island in sharply drawn white foam. Indigo water, a misty pale blue sky, cool dawn light, a warm amber glow in the lighthouse. Strong graphic shapes, gorgeous salt-worn materials, almost photographic physical detail with the controlled composition of a great illustrated book cover. Maritime, austere, extraordinary; no fantasy clutter or steampunk decoration.

The lighthouse lantern is impossibly large and has one enormous FRONT-FACING ROUND glass lens/window, with a complete simple bronze rim. Inside this lens is a smaller view of this ENTIRE SAME COMPOSITION: the whole white lighthouse with its red roof, black island, winding stair, red boathouse, ocean, foam and sky. The smaller lighthouse's round lantern contains the whole island and lighthouse again. Four readable scales repeat inward in the same orientation. It is unmistakably an image containing itself, not a tunnel of lamps and not a reflection of some unrelated scenery. The glow illuminates the very island that contains it.

Compose boldly: island fills the lower third, the lighthouse dominates the center, the round lantern window is roughly 35% of total image width, close to the center of the square. The first smaller island must be big and clear enough to recognize. Keep the full window perimeter visible with no stair, beam or object crossing it. Every repeated scene has the same proportions and motif arrangement. Spacious ocean around the isolated island, no extra islands or duplicate towers outside the recursion. Distinct white, red, black and deep blue landmarks survive as the scene shrinks. No people, faces, text, logos, collage or watermark. Avoid gold-brown fantasy palettes, fruit, circular portals floating without a physical structure, and generic polished 3D render aesthetics.

## Moon railway

Create a beautiful impossible Droste artwork, "The midnight railway". Square composition, no people, no faces.

A luminous ivory moon hangs very close in a deep ultramarine night sky. It is an enormous hollow architectural world: a broad section of its cratered shell is cut away, showing pale chalk cliffs and a tiny railway spiraling down through its interior. One bright vermilion-red train with three small cars travels along a sweeping thin viaduct. A tiny midnight-blue station, a single amber signal lamp, and a delicate track give the world scale. The train is a vivid graphic landmark, not a cartoon toy with a face. The moon fills almost the whole square; a few sparse pin-sharp stars and a distant small crescent are the only background details. Silvery moonlight, warm train windows, powdery crater texture, graceful steel rails and near-weightless bridges. Elegant and enigmatic, like a meticulously photographed physical set for a strange dream. Limit the palette to pale ivory, ink blue, and scarlet.

The cutaway exposes a very large clear ROUND opening in the moon, facing the viewer, with a crisp pale inner rim about 40% of the image width near the center. Inside this opening is the ENTIRE SAME VIEW at a smaller scale: the complete moon silhouette, its cutaway, surrounding blue sky and stars, the red train and the sweeping railway. Inside the smaller moon's opening is another complete same moon-and-railway scene, and then another. Four recognizable scales, same orientation, unmistakable picture-within-itself Droste recursion. The railway seems to descend from one scale into the next, as if the train is taking an impossible route into its own miniature universe. Keep the first nested moon fully visible and recognizable. Do not just draw a tunnel of rings or an ordinary solar system full of unrelated moons.

Give the moon a strong, asymmetrical crescent silhouette around the opening, with a wide smooth arc on one side and jagged cutaway craters on the other. Sweeping diagonal railway in the lower foreground leads the eye to the next moon. Subtle architectural precision, striking generous shapes, restrained detail. Do not reuse a fruit, a lighthouse, a telescope, brass steampunk machinery, generic sci-fi glowing portal effects, text, borders or watermark. No humans or humanoids.

## Book library

Create an extraordinary surreal Droste artwork: "A library bound inside its own book". Square image. No people, faces or animals.

One enormous open hardback book has unfolded into an impossible architectural library. The book's pale ivory pages curl upward into terraces and balconies; the page edges become hundreds of thin floor layers. Small orderly shelves of deep teal, faded coral and blue books line the curling walls. Folded paper becomes a staircase rising from the lower foreground and passing around the central opening. One bright scarlet rolling ladder and a tiny turquoise reading desk with a warm yellow lamp are memorable landmarks. The outer hardback cover is rich scarlet, visibly wrapping the base and two sides, so this whole library instantly reads as ONE OPEN BOOK. The spine is still recognizable. Beautiful paper grain, cloth binding, crisp folds, tiny shadows between pages. A sculptural impossibility, tactile and finely made.

True Droste recursion: In the middle of the open book-library is a large, perfectly clear upright rectangular opening with a simple ivory paper frame, approximately 38% of total image width and height. Through it we see a SMALLER COPY OF THE ENTIRE SAME COMPOSITION: the whole red-covered open book, all its curling library floors, staircase, scarlet ladder, desk, surrounding pale atmosphere, and the central opening. Inside that smaller library's opening is another whole same book-library, repeating inward through four clear scales. Same orientation at every level. The whole book contains the whole library which contains the whole book. Make this specific image-within-itself recursion unmistakable; do not merely show a deep library corridor, progressively smaller shelves, a blank book, illegible page text, or unrelated books scattered around.

Composition is nearly frontal with just enough depth to see the curling shelves. Wide graceful curls rise asymmetrically around the opening; the lower paper stair leads the eye toward its smaller counterpart. A bright spacious background of warm off-white and very pale blue, gentle daylight and long soft shadows. Airy rather than dark or sepia. Broad ivory, scarlet and teal shapes should read at thumbnail size, with intimate detail rewarded up close. No gold brass decoration, steampunk machinery, fruit, stars, circular portal effects, people, cartoon styling, writing, typography, labels, logos, picture border or watermark.
