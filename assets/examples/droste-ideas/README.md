# Droste concept studies

Eight alternatives to the pear observatory, generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are concept studies; the live demo remains on the pear while the alternatives are explored.

1. [The lighthouse that lights itself](lighthouse.png): its lantern contains its whole island.
2. [The midnight railway](moon-railway.png): a cutaway moon whose railway leads into a smaller copy of the same moon.
3. [A library bound inside its own book](book-library.png): pages become floors, stairs and shelves; the opening contains the whole book again.
4. [The ocean in a raindrop](raindrop-aquarium.png): a shell inside a floating aquarium contains the entire water drop again.
5. [The waterfall that feeds itself](mountain-waterfall.png): a snowy mountain pours water into a smaller copy of its own landscape.
6. [The theatre performing itself](recursive-theatre.png): an empty cutaway opera house stages the whole theatre again.
7. [The garden that grows its own time](clock-garden.png): a pocket watch holds a garden whose conservatory contains the whole watch.
8. [A city sailing into its own harbour](paper-boat-city.png): a folded-paper coastal city shelters a smaller boat carrying the same city.

Each artwork is saved in this directory. The complete generation prompts are in [prompts.md](prompts.md) for the first three and [prompts-02.md](prompts-02.md) for the next five.
