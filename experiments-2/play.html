Repeating the picture gives us the Droste effect. Unrolling it turns the nested copies into a repeating strip. Tilt that strip just enough to connect one level to the next, then roll it back up. The resulting spiral is a tententoon.

The angle isn’t a lucky guess. It comes from how much each copy shrinks. Here, it’s a few degrees.
